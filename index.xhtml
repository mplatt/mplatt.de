I hold a PhD in Computer Science from King’s College London, where I researched identity-based consensus protocols supervised by Prof. Peter McBurney. My academic path also includes degrees from TU Berlin and Birkbeck, University of London.
I currently serve as a Technology Manager at Google where I also carry out research on occasion. While my primary focus is on industry work, I remain open to academic collaborations, particularly those related to blockchain and payment systems. From time to time, I contribute to discussions on these topics at academic or professional events.
If you're interested in collaboration or would like to get in touch, please feel free to reach out.
